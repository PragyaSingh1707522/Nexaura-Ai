# CUAD v1 attribution

Dataset: Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD) v1, curated by The Atticus Project.

Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/theatticusproject/cuad

License stated by the dataset publisher: CC BY 4.0. The copied annotation JSON is retained for reproducible local training; consult the source dataset datasheet and license before redistribution or commercial use.

Scope limitation: CUAD labels important commercial-contract review clauses. It is not a regulatory obligations dataset. Results on this corpus do not establish regulatory clause classification accuracy.
